Where Sibyl Memory is actually differentiated—and where it is not

The current build uses memory on a critical path, but most of its provider-risk state is a derived view over public chain outcomes plus local deliverable artifacts. That passes a dependency demonstration; it is not yet a durable product moat.

Market shape and moat

Crowdedness: moderate-to-crowded. Public reputation, attestations and escrow arbitration have multiple mature primitives. The realistic moat is a compounding proprietary incident corpus plus integrations that convert those incidents into task-specific controls. A provider score alone is easy to clone and vulnerable to Sybil/reset behavior.

Recommended differentiation: evolve from “an evaluator that remembers bad providers” into “a preventive control-plane that remembers how work failed, then rewrites acceptance tests, milestone size, evidence requirements and escalation policy for the next job.” The irreducible memory is semantic and contextual; the chain remains the public settlement and commitment layer.
